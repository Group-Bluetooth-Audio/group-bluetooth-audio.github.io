Group Bluetooth Audio

Powerful multi-device Bluetooth audio management for macOS. Connect unlimited speakers and headphones simultaneously, control individual volumes, and distribute audio seamlessly across your setup. This activated version delivers full core functionality without restrictions.

Unlimited Device Connections

Pair and manage as many Bluetooth audio devices as needed—headphones, speakers, soundbars. No limits, no compromises. The app creates system-level aggregate devices for instant multi-output playback.

Precise Individual Control

Adjust volume levels independently for each connected device using precision sliders. Real-time monitoring shows connection status and audio levels in a clean, native interface.

Advanced Audio Synchronization

Built on CoreAudio with drift correction for perfect timing across devices. Integrates seamlessly with macOS Audio MIDI Setup, ensuring compatibility with any app—from Spotify to Final Cut Pro.

Effortless Menu Bar Access

Quick controls and status monitoring directly from your menu bar. Smart detection categorizes devices instantly, with visual indicators for active connections and issues.

Native macOS Design

Modern split-view layout provides intuitive access to selection and controls. Real-time feedback keeps your audio setup transparent and reliable.

How It Works

Step 1: Connect Devices

Pair your Bluetooth speakers and headphones via System Settings. Group Bluetooth Audio detects them automatically.

Step 2: Build Aggregate Device

Select desired devices in the app. It creates a multi-output aggregate device that appears system-wide.

Step 3: Adjust and Play

Fine-tune individual volumes with sliders. Route audio from any app to your grouped setup.

Step 4: Monitor from Menu Bar

Access real-time status, quick controls, and fixes directly from the menu bar.
